Weight

Heavier walkers are bigger, step slower, shorter and lower, take longer to get going and to stop, stomp the ground (a shockwave from 80 t), barely hop, flinch less and turn their turret slower. Lighter ones are the reverse.

Upper body and loadout

The upper body (turret, cockpit, weapons) sits high and a little forward, so a heavier one raises the center of mass, tips the walker more, twists more slowly and takes longer to stop.

Main cannon

Each shot pushes back along the barrel at the muzzle's height. A tank gun is about 0.55. Two feet in a line have only their length to resist it, so a biped rocks hard; deploy first to brace.

Turret aim

Cursor makes the turret follow the mouse over the ground while the body keeps moving.

Movement
Feet

With glue off, feet follow the gait curve and slide when speed changes. Useful to compare.

Jump jets

The legs push off, the jets burn, it coasts to the top, then a retro burn fires as late as it can and still land the walker slowly enough for its legs to absorb. Above 1× its weight, rockets can lift even a walker whose legs cannot jump.
