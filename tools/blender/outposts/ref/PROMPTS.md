# Grok prompts — outpost rig heads with no concept art

The six outpost buildings are modelled from the owner's concept image (`docs/16 Bulwark MM/design/outpost/
outpost-concept.jpg`, crops in this folder). Five of the six mining-rig extraction heads (design §8.4) have no art.
Run each prompt in Grok **with the concept image attached** as the style reference, and save the result here as
`rig_<head>.jpg` (`rig_auger.jpg`, `rig_brine.jpg`, `rig_crystal.jpg`, `rig_bore.jpg`, `rig_shielded.jpg`).

Verdance's three rigs come first: **Auger Drill** (iron) and **Brine Pump** (lithium). The Laser Cutter (aluminum)
is the rig in the concept image. The other three heads are needed for the later planets.

Every prompt shares this style block (it is repeated in each, so each prompt stands alone):

> Same art style, camera and lighting as the attached outpost image: isometric three-quarter view from above,
> stylised low-poly hard-surface sci-fi, chunky readable shapes, light grey and white hull panels over dark grey
> chamfered bases, orange trim stripes and fins, cyan-blue glowing lights and power conduits. One building only,
> centred, standing on a single square stone build pad on cracked brown rock, plain soft grey background, no text.

## 1. Auger Drill — iron (Verdance, common)

> A mining rig for a sci-fi outpost: an Auger Drill. A heavy square drill tower on a chamfered base, with a huge
> vertical screw auger driving down into a rust-red iron ore outcrop of dark metallic chunks. A short conveyor belt
> carries broken red-brown ore chunks from the drill head into a small tracked ore cart. Hydraulic pistons on the
> sides, a cyan glowing power socket at the back, orange hazard stripes on the drill housing. Rock chips and dust
> spraying where the auger bites. [style block]

## 2. Brine Pump — lithium (Verdance, rare)

> A mining rig for a sci-fi outpost: a Brine Pump. A pump-jack derrick with a rocking beam over a wellhead, two
> cylindrical storage tanks with glowing pale cyan-white fluid level windows, and a row of shallow evaporation trays
> where the brine dries into white and pale pink lithium salt crystals. Pipes run from the wellhead to the tanks and
> the trays. Orange valve wheels and hazard stripes, a cyan glowing power socket. [style block]

## 3. Crystal Harvester — silicon / yttrium / iridium

> A mining rig for a sci-fi outpost: a Crystal Harvester. Three tall articulated resonator arms with glowing tips
> surround a cluster of large blue-grey crystals growing out of the rock; shards break off and float on a shimmering
> beam into a domed collector on a chamfered base. Faint concentric resonance rings in the air around the crystals.
> Orange trim on the arms, cyan glow on the collector. [style block]

## 4. Deep Bore — cobalt / gallium / tungsten / vanadium / platinum / palladium / europium

> A mining rig for a sci-fi outpost: a Deep Bore. A tall lattice derrick tower over a round borehole, a winch
> lowering a long cylindrical core barrel on a cable, and a work deck beside it where long rock core samples with
> deep blue cobalt veins lie split open in racks. A small crane arm on the deck. Orange stripes on the derrick,
> cyan lights up the tower. [style block]

## 5. Shielded Extractor — uranium

> A mining rig for a sci-fi outpost: a Shielded Extractor. A low armoured dome with thick sealed panels and amber
> warning beacons, a heavy shielded hatch on one side where two robotic arms load lead-grey canisters with small
> green glowing windows onto a rail cart. Radiation trefoil warning signs as simple shapes, orange hazard stripes,
> cyan power glow at the base. [style block]
