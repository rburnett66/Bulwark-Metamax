Bulwark · Farming models

Built in headless Blender 5.2: flat shaded, no textures. Verdance outpost ground, 1 square = 1 battle cell (4 model units). A farm plot is 2 × 2 cells, 4 rows, the area of 4 Machine Guns. The ground here is a stand-in: in game it is the outpost map's own terrain.

Tap a plot: your avatar (the ship's gold hard-hat figure) walks to it and works it on: Prepped → Planted → Sprout → Plant → Fruited. Fruited harvests (embers in the pod's colour); Dead gives the pods back. Taps queue. The farmhands are the ship's Verdance crew.
