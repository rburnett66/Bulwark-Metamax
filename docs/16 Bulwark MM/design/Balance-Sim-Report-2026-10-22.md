# Balance Simulation Report: new faction maps and unit balance (2026-10-22)

**Asked (owner, 2026-10-22):** "redesign all maps for level 1 other than powder … then run simulations on unit balancing and share results."
**Tracked:** [MM-story_mvjw67lo_f]. Maps: `content/maps/forge/<slug>-map-1.json`, recipes and guardrails in `tools/maps/faction-maps.mjs`. Spec: [Faction-Terrain-Design](Faction-Terrain-Design.md) §1 and §2.3.

---

## 1. Summary

1. **Every faction now plays its own planet on map 1.** Eight new maps, each built from its planet recipe, all inside the fairness guardrails. Powder is unchanged.
2. **Two unit-balance faults outweigh anything a map does:**
   - **Dark Energy and Greenies cannot hurt the base or towers.** 7 of their 8 units deal Poison damage, and Poison does **0× damage to Structure** (`tables.js` `EFFECTIVENESS`).
   - **Missiles out-range every anti-air tower.** All six factions' Missiles fire from 8.75–10.9 cells. Flak reaches 6.5 and the Missile Tower 8. They hover out of reach, and in the first air wave they deal about 3,000–9,000 damage, more than the base's 3,000 HP. If the base can't die, the wave never ends.
3. **Maps change ground pressure strongly in waves 1–2.**
   - The open maps (Ochre for High Tech, Lumen for Space Tech) let **Artillery** set up out of tower range. HTC-Artillery reaches 11.2 and SPC-Artillery 11, both beyond the Rocket tower's 10. The result is about 10× the early base damage of the Powder layout.
   - Cinder (Arcane) gives about 2.6×, Kessra (Artillery) about 1.3×, and Florael (Air) about the same as Powder.
4. **The unit pricing harness (`balanceSim.js`) is not usable as it stands.**
   - It measures 46 of the 73 units at **0 DPS**: every aircraft, plus the Poison units, which is the same 0× fault.
   - The units it does measure come out at about 0.15–0.33× their table price.
   - Its numbers should not drive a retune until it is fixed.

**Guardrails on every map:**
- 12–24% impassable;
- no ground spawn cut off;
- a second ground route survives if the shortest one is blocked;
- under 3% of open ground sealed off;

**On Rime:** swimming saves water units distance from 76% of ground spawns. The water route averages 0.74 of the ground route. This only shows in play once the water movement rules ([MM-story_mvj70e3w_6]) are built.

- Lumen still uses the borrowed green palette until the `lunar` palette (§5.1) lands.

## 3. How the simulation was run

**Map impact.** Each faction plays its 8 campaign waves on its **old** map 1 (the Powder copy) and its **new** map 1. Everything uses the live sim code: `createSim`, `stepSim`, `applyCommand`, deterministic.

**Scripted player (the same on every map):**
- every second: sends the idle harvester to the nearest field, spends all gold, upgrades with what's left;
- **builds in rotation:** Cannon (close in), Missile Tower (4–6 cells out), Rockets (close in), Flak (4–6 cells out);
- 8 runs per map, each starting its search round the base at a different place.

**Two studies:**
- **Survival:** normal base HP. Measures wins and waves survived.
- **Pressure:** the base can't die and a wave stuck for 4 minutes is cleared, so all 8 waves play out. Measures base damage by lane (ground, water, air) and the time to the first ground hit.

**Unit pricing.** The existing harness, `runPricingReport(all 73 units, 25 battles)`.

**Limits:**
- one scripted player, not a human;
- the sim is deterministic, so the spread between runs comes only from where the bot builds;
- results describe **map 1 only**.

## 4. Results: maps

### 4.1 Survival (8 runs per map)

| Faction | Wins, old map | Wins, new map | Waves survived, old → new |
|---|---|---|---|
| Powder | 1/8 | 1/8 | 4.1 → 4.1 (unchanged map) |
| Air | 4/8 | 4/8 | 5.1 → 5.3 |
| High Tech | 0/8 | 0/8 | 2.8 → **2.0** |
| Artillery | 0/8 | 0/8 | 2.0 → 2.3 |
| Water | 1/8 | 0/8 | 1.3 → 1.0 |
| Arcane / Energy | 0/8 | 0/8 | 3.8 → 3.3 |
| Space Tech | 0/8 | 0/8 | 2.8 → 2.5 |
| Dark Energy | 0/8 | 1/8 | 4.9 → 3.6 |
| Greenies | 6/8 | 7/8 | 6.6 → 7.4 |

**What survival shows:**
- Survival is decided almost everywhere by the **first air wave**: wave 3, or wave 1 for Water. That happens on both maps, so the map barely moves it (§5.2).
- Dark Energy and Greenies "lose" only because of waves that never end, not because of base damage (§5.1).

### 4.2 Ground pressure: waves 1–2, the all-ground waves (base damage, mean of 8 runs)

| Faction | Old map | New map | Change | Main damage dealer on the new map |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Powder | 209 | 209 | — | GND-Tanks |
| Air | 685 | 766 | +12% | AIR-Trucks, AIR-Troops |
| **High Tech** | 396 | **4,176** | **×10.5** | HTC-Artillery (529 → 4,323 over all waves) |
| Artillery | 1,156 | 1,525 | +32% | ART-Artillery |
| Water | 0 | 38 | — | waves 1–2 are mostly air |
| **Arcane / Energy** | 452 | **1,186** | **×2.6** | ARC-Artillery, ARC-Tanks |
| **Space Tech** | 95 | **1,058** | **×11** | SPC-Artillery (0 → 872) |
| Dark Energy | 0 | 236 | — | DRK-Artillery (Concussion, the one non-Poison unit) |
| Greenies | 0 | 0 | — | Poison, 0× vs Structure |

**Why:**
- Artillery range varies by faction: ART 12.5, HTC 11.2, SPC 11, ARC 10.2, GND 10, DRK 10, AIR 9.8, WTR 9.8, GRN 9.5. The towers reach Rockets 10, Missile Tower 8, Cannon 4.5. So High Tech's and Space Tech's artillery out-range every tower, and Arcane's slightly out-ranges Rockets.
- On open maps (Ochre, Lumen) artillery reaches a firing position with a clear line and sits outside every tower's reach.
- That matches the design intent ("long sightlines for the gunners"), but a tenfold rise in the opening waves of map 1 is too punishing for a first map.

**Time to first ground hit**, the mean across all waves, old → new:
- Space Tech: 21.6 → 37.1 s
- Water: 36.9 → 45.2 s
- Artillery: 54.5 → 61.4 s
- High Tech: 55.0 → 46.5 s

Bigger obstacles mostly lengthen the approach. Ochre's open desert shortens it.

## 5. Results: unit balance

### 5.1 Poison does nothing to buildings (Dark Energy, Greenies)

- `EFFECTIVENESS.Poison = { Organic 1.8, Machinery 0.1, Aircraft 0.1, Structure 0, Energy 0 }`.
- The base and every tower are Structure.
- **Dark Energy:** 7 of 8 units are Poison. The other is DRK-Artillery, which deals Concussion.
- **Greenies:** 7 of 8 units are Poison.
- **In the sims:** Greenies dealt **0** base damage across all 8 waves on both maps. Dark Energy dealt 0 on the old map and 236 (artillery only) on the new one.
- Their "losses" are waves that never end: units that can't hurt anything stand at the base forever.

### 5.2 Missiles out-range all anti-air

| Unit | Range | | Tower | Range | Hits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GND / DRK-Missiles | 8.75 | | Cannon | 4.5 | ground |
| ARC-Missiles | 8.93 | | Flak | 6.5 | air |
| SPC-Missiles | 9.63 | | Missile Tower | 8.0 | air |
| HTC-Missiles | 9.80 | | Rockets | 10.0 | ground |
| ART-Missiles | 10.94 | | | | |

**The effect:**
- Missiles stop at their range from the base and fire.
- Anti-air next to the base can't reach them. Anti-air pushed 4–6 cells out reaches some of them.
- They are the top damage dealer for every faction that has them. Over the pressure study:
  - GND-Missiles 24,300 vs 1,221 for the next unit;
  - ART-Missiles 93,798.
- When the base can't die, a missile wave never clears. This is the stall behind the existing ticket [MM-task_mvgz4dxd_3] ("copter attacks base from out of range").

### 5.3 Artillery out-ranges the longest tower

- Four factions' Artillery out-range the Rocket tower (10): ART 12.5, HTC 11.2, SPC 11, ARC 10.2. The rest are at or under it: GND 10, DRK 10, AIR 9.8, WTR 9.8, GRN 9.5.
- On open maps this decides the early waves (§4.2).
- *Correction:* an earlier draft of this report said every faction's Artillery had range 12.5. Only the Artillery faction's does.

### 5.4 The pricing harness

`runPricingReport` (25 battles per unit, all 73 units) gives:
- **0 DPS for 46 units:** every Flyer in every faction, plus the Dark Energy and Greenies Poison units (§5.1).
- **Prices for the rest at 0.15–0.33× the table price:**

| Unit | Measured price ÷ table price |
|---|---|
| AIR-HeavyBomber | 0.33 |
| Artillery units, all factions | 0.21–0.32 |
| Troops | 0.15–0.18 |

**Diagnosis:**
- The harness board, or the way it routes flyers, no longer matches the game.
- Its gold-per-DPS calibration is about 3× off.
- Until both are fixed, its prices should not be used for retuning.

## 6. Recommended fixes

Each is a data change in `tables.js`, apart from the harness. Each needs a `balanceSim` check once the harness is fixed. **Not applied in this change**: balance is your call.

1. **Poison vs Structure.** Give Poison a small Structure value (for example 0.4), or give each Poison faction a non-Poison siege unit, so Dark Energy and Greenies can threaten the base. → [MM-task_mvjxae69_g]
2. **Missiles vs anti-air.** Bring Missiles' range under the Missile Tower's (for example ≤ 7.5), or raise the Missile Tower to about 11. Also make attackers that cannot reach anything move in, ending the wave stall. → [MM-task_mvjxagdi_h] (with [MM-task_mvgz4dxd_3])
3. **Artillery vs the longest tower.** Trim Artillery to about 9.5 (under Rockets' 10), or let Rockets out-reach it, so open maps reward sightlines without a tenfold opening spike. → [MM-task_mvjxahqe_i]
4. **The pricing harness.** Fix flyer routing on the harness board and recalibrate gold per DPS against today's roster, then re-run the full pass (100 battles per unit). → [MM-task_mvjxajem_j]

After 1–3, re-run this report's map study. Reproduce with `node tools/maps/map-balance-sim.mjs <baseline.json> 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8 out.json` and `node tools/maps/map-balance-report.mjs out.json`. The map comparison will then measure the maps rather than the two range faults.

## 8. Addendum: the Machine Gun tower (PR #123) [MM-story_mvk3kwu2_m]

The owner asked on 2026-10-22: "re-run balancing with machine gun at lowest tower pricing". The Machine Gun already costs the lowest price of any gun: 150 / 375 / 750, the same as a Mine Drone. Only the Wall is cheaper, at 100.

**Method.** Same harness and scripted player as §3 (`tools/maps/map-balance-sim.mjs`). Each faction's own map 1, 5 seeds, with this PR and the Toxic/artillery change (#120) both applied. Three build plans:
- **No MG:** Cannon, Missile, Rockets, Flak, as before.
- **MG as a fifth tower:** Cannon, Missile, Rockets, Flak, MG (`MG=add`).
- **MG-heavy:** MG, Cannon, Missile, MG, Rockets, Flak (`MG=1`).

| Faction | Wins, no MG | Wins, MG fifth | Wins, MG-heavy | Waves 1–2 base damage (no MG → fifth → heavy) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground / Powder | 1/5 | 0/5 | 0/5 | 187 → 100 → 237 |
| Air | 4/5 | **5/5** | 4/5 | 682 → 879 → 994 |
| High Tech | 0/5 | 0/5 | 0/5 | 1955 → 1231 → 1215 |
| Artillery | 0/5 | 0/5 | 0/5 | 120 → 35 → 83 |
| Water | 0/5 | **4/5** | 3/5 | **9863 → 2522 → 2455** |
| Arcane / Energy | 0/5 | 0/5 | 0/5 | 975 → 781 → 690 |
| Space Tech | 0/5 | 0/5 | 0/5 | 311 → 267 → 524 |
| Dark Energy | 1/5 | 0/5 | 0/5 | 13 → 0 → 0 (runs stall, see below) |
| Greenies | 5/5 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 0 → 0 → 0 |

**What it shows**

- **Water is the big change, from 0/5 to 4/5.** Water's first waves are mostly aircraft (WTR-Copters and WTR-Planes were its top damage dealers), and its troops and tanks are Swimmers, which the Cannon and Rocket towers cannot target. The Machine Gun hits both, so close-in defence finally has a cheap answer to them.
- **Early waves get easier almost everywhere.** In waves 1–2, base damage falls for High Tech, Artillery, Arcane and Water, because the Machine Gun shreds troops and aircraft at 150 gold.
- **The losses after wave 2 are almost all Missile Flyers (finding 5.2).** They park outside every anti-air range. In the pressure runs, missile damage rises from about 21k to 95–107k on Powder and from 23k to 168–185k on Arcane. The Machine Gun's 5-cell range cannot reach the standoff, and any gold it takes from Missile Towers lets the standoff grind longer. The Machine Gun does not fix that finding; it needs the missile-range fix.
- **Dark Energy:** its runs stall (a wave still alive after 240 s), so its win counts measure the stall rule, not the tower. Greenies win 5/5 in every plan.

**Verdict.** At 150 gold the Machine Gun is not overpowered. It is the right early and anti-Swimmer pick, and its price can stay. Overall balance still hinges on the Missile Flyer standoff (task_mvjxagdi_h), not on the Machine Gun.

## 9. Addendum: Missile Flyers become Drones [MM-story_mvk7mn6z_q]

The owner said on 2026-10-22: "The drone is the lightest attack aircraft … a dive bomber that hits once and explodes, or a drone could carry a machine gun." The owner's calls:
- Missile Flyer becomes the drone, with both kinds split per faction.
- Fighters stay as they are.
- The Bomber stays Air-only.

**Applied:**
- **Kamikaze drones** (Artillery, Arcane, Dark, Greenies) fly at 2.6. On contact they explode once, as a Concussion burst of 6 s of their old fire within 1 cell, and die. A drone that blows itself up pays no bounty.
- **MG drones** (Ground, Air, High Tech, Water, Space) fly at 2.2 and strafe from 3.5 cells at 65% of their old fire.
- HP and cost are unchanged.
- Every anti-air tower out-ranges both kinds.

**Re-run.** Same harness, each faction's own map 1, 5 seeds, the "MG as a fifth tower" plan, against main as it was before this change:

| Faction | Wins: missiles → drones | Waves survived | Pressure base damage | Drone damage | Top damage dealer now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground / Powder | 0/5 → **5/5** | 2.4 → 8.0 | 95,139 → 286 | 94,834 → 2 | GND-Planes |
| Air | 5/5 → 5/5 | 8.0 → 8.0 | 2,185 → 2,185 | 0 → 0 (no drones in its waves) | AIR-Trucks |
| High Tech | 0/5 → **3/5** | 2.0 → 6.0 | 90,585 → 7,441 | 84,888 → 34 | HTC-Artillery |
| Artillery | 0/5 → 0/5 | 2.2 → 4.6 | 190,839 → 24,466 | 163,779 → 0 | ART-Planes |
| Water | 4/5 → 4/5 | 7.2 → 7.2 | 3,149 → 3,149 | 0 → 0 | WTR-Copters |
| Arcane / Energy | 0/5 → **4/5** | 2.2 → 7.0 | 165,938 → 3,333 | 163,009 → 87 | ARC-Planes |
| Space Tech | 0/5 → **4/5** | 2.0 → 6.8 | 86,725 → 3,933 | 84,203 → 96 | SPC-Planes |
| Dark Energy | 0/5 → **5/5** | 2.0 → 8.0 | 22 → 22 | 0 → 0 | DRK-Artillery |
| Greenies | 5/5 → 5/5 | 8.0 → 8.0 | 0 → 0 | 0 → 0 | — |

**What it shows**

- **The missile standoff is gone.** It was the single biggest source of base damage, about 85k–165k per faction in the pressure runs. Survival now tracks the units that should decide it: Fighters (Planes), Artillery and Copters.
- **Dark Energy's stalls are gone.** Its 0/5 came from a missile wave that could never be cleared, and it now plays out to 5/5.
- **Drones are now too easy to kill.** With their old 82–112 HP and fast but direct flight, the scripted player's anti-air downs almost every drone before it attacks: 0–96 damage per run. As a threat they went from overwhelming to negligible.

**Suggested next tune (not applied; your call):** make drones dangerous in numbers instead of individually.
- Double kamikaze HP, or have kamikazes arrive in swarms of 3, so a few get through.
- Raise MG drone HP by about 1.5× so they live long enough to strafe.

Artillery (0/5) is now limited by ART-Planes and its own artillery, not by drones.
