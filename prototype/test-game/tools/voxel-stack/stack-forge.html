Sensitivity: higher removes more background (looser match to the seed colour), lower keeps more edge pixels. Saturated subject colours are protected, so you can raise it to erase stubborn off-white without eating (e.g.) green leaves. 🎯 Pick colour: click the button, then click any background colour in the image to knock it out — click several tints to remove them all (each ringed marker is one pick). Zoom: Shift + scroll magnifies toward the cursor up to 80× so single pixels are trimmable on 4K art. Polygons: click points, click the first to close; Keep shapes union into the subject, Cut shapes remove what's inside. Esc backs out of the current mode. The magenta line is the final cutout outline.
