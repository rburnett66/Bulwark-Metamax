Styles: Neon voxel, Synthwave dusk, Ember and Hologram are presets of one shader (data only). Neon voxel: dark block faces, glowing block edges, a cell grid on the ground, glowing roads and water, outlined units, and a cheap glow pass (Low tier skips it). Daylight is the same scene lit by the sun. Lit 3D plateaus, rocks and trees under one light. Static shadows render once into a shadow map; unit shadows are live. Ground and roads are one baked image per level. Units are the real GND-Tanks sprite pack, facing the camera.
