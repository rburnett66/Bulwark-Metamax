UNIT ART

Reference art for every unit in the roster. Drop images into a unit's slots; they are written to content/units/refs/ and modelled from there.

Drop several files on a card at once: names containing top / side / front / rear (back) and base (chassis, hull) / turret (weapon, gun) / head (radar, dish, sensor) are sorted into the right slots. Anything else goes to the sheet slot.

Pick a unit to see its art slots.
Each unit has a whole-unit row (top, side, front, rear — side and top are needed to model it), optional rows for its base, turret and head on their own, and a slot for a combined sheet.
